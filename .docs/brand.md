Foundations:

1. Brand Purpose: The reason the brand exsist beyond making money, mission, beliefs, emotional anchor

2. Brand Positioning: How the brand wants to be seen compared to competitors, its unique space.

3. Brand Personality: The human-like traits of the brand 

4. Brand Story/Narrative: The authentic backstory and ongoing story that connects emotionally with people.

Visual and Verbal components:

1. Brand Name

2. Logo & Symbols

3. Color Palette

4. Typography

5. Imagery & Photographic style

6. Tone, Voice and Messaging {Tagline slogan}
